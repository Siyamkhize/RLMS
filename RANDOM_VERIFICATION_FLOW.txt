KEY FEATURES:
=============
✅ Random selection prevents gaming
✅ Works when app is minimized
✅ Phone vibration + notifications
✅ Cannot dismiss until verified
✅ Countdown timer with color coding
✅ Detailed compliance tracking
✅ User-friendly error messages
✅ Offline support with local storage

TIMING:

• Check frequency: Every 30 seconds

• Vibration pattern: 500ms-1000ms-500ms-1000ms
• Notification: Persistent (cannot dismiss)

SECURITY:
=========
