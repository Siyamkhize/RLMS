  STEP 2: SUM-BASED CALCULATION PER UNIT STANDARD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ WRONG METHOD (AVG of individual percentages):
   (30% + 25% + 40%) / 3 = 31.67%
   
   Problem: Treats all exercises equally regardless of weight!
   A 10-mark question has same impact as 20-mark question.

✅ CORRECT METHOD (SUM then percentage):
   
   Total Scored:    3 + 5 + 6 = 14
   Total Possible: 10 + 20 + 15 = 45
   
   Percentage: (14 / 45) × 100 = 31.11%
   
   Benefit: Correctly weights exercises by their total marks!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  IMPLEMENTATION IN CODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SQL Query (Line 309 & 363 in get_learners_with_poe_assigned.php):

SELECT 
    m.learnerID,
    CAST(REGEXP_SUBSTR(m.exercise, '[0-9]{4,5}') AS UNSIGNED) as unit_standard_id,
    (SUM(m.marks_scored) / SUM(a.marks)) * 100 as unit_standard_percentage
    ↑                       ↑
    │                       │
    └─ SUM all marks scored │
                            └─ SUM all total marks
FROM marks m
LEFT JOIN assessments a ON m.exercise = a.exercise
WHERE a.assessment_type = 'Summative'
GROUP BY m.learnerID, unit_standard_id
         ↑
         └─ Calculate ONE percentage per unit standard
