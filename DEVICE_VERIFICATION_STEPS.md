## 📱 ON-DEVICE TESTING STEPS

### Step 1: Prepare the Device (2 minutes)

1. **Unlock phone** and go to home screen
2. **Open Settings**
3. Navigate to: **Settings → Apps → RLMSS**

7. Go back to home screen

1. Tap **RLMSS** app icon
2. Wait for app to fully load
3. You should see login screen or home page

### Step 3: Navigate to Bricklayer Toolkit (2 minutes)

1. Enter facilitator username/password
2. Tap Login
3. Wait for dashboard to load

**From Dashboard:**
1. Look for **ARPL** or **Assessment** section
2. Find **Bricklayer Toolkit** option
3. Tap to open
4. Select a learner (e.g., Learner 70 or available learner)
5. Click **Bricklayer Toolkit Assessment**

### Step 4: Check Appendix E Tab (2 minutes)

1. Look at the top of the screen - should see **tabs**
2. Find and tap the **Appendix E** tab
3. **Wait 1-2 seconds** for content to load

## 🚨 IF SOMETHING'S WRONG

### Issue: Still Shows Empty

**Solution:**
1. Go to Settings → Apps → RLMSS
2. Clear Cache (again)
3. Clear Data (if cache alone doesn't work)
4. Force Stop
5. Completely restart phone (power off → power on)
6. Open app again

### Issue: App Crashes

**Solution:**
1. Force Stop app
2. Clear Cache
3. Clear Data
4. Restart phone
5. Open app fresh

### Issue: Takes Too Long to Load

**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. If on WiFi, switch to mobile data (or vice versa)
3. Try again
