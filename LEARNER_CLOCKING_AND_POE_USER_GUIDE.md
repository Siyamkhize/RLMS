# Learner Clocking & Document Scanning - User Guide

## What These Features Do

### Learner Clocking Feature
This feature helps track when learners arrive and leave their training sessions. Think of it like a digital attendance register that uses fingerprints instead of signatures.

### POE (Portfolio of Evidence) Feature
This feature lets you scan paper documents with your phone's camera and turn them into digital PDF files that get saved in the system.

---

## How Learner Clocking Works

### The Simple Process
1. **Find Your Name** - Look for your name in the list of learners
2. **Scan Your Finger** - Place your finger on the scanner device
3. **Wait for Confirmation** - The system checks if it's really you
4. **Done!** - You're now clocked in or out

### What Happens Behind the Scenes
- The system checks you're at the right location (within 50 meters of the training site)
- It compares your fingerprint to the one stored in the system
- It records the exact time you arrived or left
- Everything gets saved both on the tablet and sent to the main server

### When Things Go Wrong

#### "Scanner Not Connected"
**What it means:** The fingerprint scanner isn't working properly
**What to do:** 
- Check if the USB cable is plugged in properly
- Ask your facilitator to restart the scanner
- Try using a different USB port

#### "You're Too Far Away"
**What it means:** You're not close enough to the training location
**What to do:**
- Move closer to the main building or training area
- Make sure you're within 50 meters of the site
- If you're sure you're in the right place, ask your facilitator for help

#### "Fingerprint Doesn't Match"
**What it means:** The system can't recognize your fingerprint
**What to do:**
- Clean your finger and try again
- Try using a different finger (if you enrolled multiple fingers)
- Press firmly but don't move your finger during scanning
- If it still doesn't work, you may need to re-enroll your fingerprints

#### "Already Clocked In"
**What it means:** You've already recorded your arrival today
**What to do:** This is normal - you can only clock in once per day

## How Document Scanning (POE) Works

### The Simple Process
1. **Select Your Name** - Choose yourself from the learner list
2. **Start Scanning** - Tap the "Start Scanning" button
3. **Scan Your Pages** - Use your phone camera to scan each page of your document
4. **Review & Upload** - Check the scanned document and upload it

### Tips for Better Scanning

#### Before You Start
- **Good Lighting:** Scan near a window or under bright lights
- **Flat Surface:** Place documents on a flat, dark surface
- **Clean Camera:** Wipe your phone's camera lens clean

#### While Scanning
- **Hold Steady:** Keep your phone still while scanning
- **Fill the Frame:** Make sure the document fills most of the camera view
- **Avoid Shadows:** Don't let your phone cast shadows on the document
- **Scan Continuously:** Don't take long breaks between pages

#### Page Limits
- **Maximum 100 pages per batch** - The scanner will crash if you try to scan too many pages at once
- **For large documents:** Scan in smaller batches (e.g., pages 1-100, then 101-200)

### When Things Go Wrong

#### "Scanner Session Lost"
**What it means:** The scanning stopped unexpectedly
**What to do:**
- This usually happens when scanning too many pages at once
- Restart the scanning process
- Scan fewer pages in each batch (50-80 pages maximum)

#### "Blurry Images"
**What it means:** The scanned pages are hard to read
**What to do:**
- Make sure you have good lighting
- Hold the phone steady while scanning
- Clean the camera lens
- Scan again if the quality is poor

#### "File Too Large"
**What it means:** Your document is too big to upload
**What to do:**
- Scan fewer pages per batch
- The system has a 150MB limit per document

#### "Upload Failed"
**What it means:** The document couldn't be sent to the server
**What to do:**
- Check your internet connection
- Try uploading again
- If it keeps failing, ask your facilitator for help

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Internet Connection Issues

#### When You're Offline
- **Clocking still works** - Your attendance gets saved on the tablet
- **Scanning still works** - Documents get saved locally
- **Automatic sync** - Everything uploads when internet comes back

#### What You See
- "Saved locally (will sync when online)" - This is normal and means your data is safe

### Scanner Hardware Problems

#### If the Fingerprint Scanner Stops Working
1. **Check connections** - Make sure USB cable is plugged in
2. **Restart the app** - Close and reopen the attendance app
3. **Try different fingers** - Use the finger you enrolled with
4. **Clean the scanner** - Wipe the scanner surface gently

#### If Scanning Keeps Failing
- **Alternative method:** Ask your facilitator about backup authentication (PIN + photo)
- **Re-enrollment:** You might need to register your fingerprints again

### Location Problems

#### GPS Not Working
- **Move outdoors** - GPS works better outside
- **Wait a moment** - Give the phone time to find your location
- **Check permissions** - Make sure the app can access your location

## Best Practices

### For Learner Clocking
1. **Arrive early** - Give yourself time in case of technical issues
2. **Keep fingers clean** - Dirt and moisture affect fingerprint scanning
3. **Be patient** - Wait for the system to confirm before leaving
4. **Report problems** - Tell your facilitator if something isn't working

### For Document Scanning
1. **Prepare documents** - Organize pages before starting
2. **Good environment** - Find a well-lit, quiet space
3. **Batch scanning** - Don't try to scan everything at once
4. **Check quality** - Review scanned pages before uploading
5. **Upload immediately** - Don't wait - upload each batch right away

### General Tips
1. **Keep the app updated** - Make sure you have the latest version
2. **Charge your device** - Scanning uses a lot of battery
3. **Free up storage** - Make sure your device has enough space
4. **Ask for help** - Don't struggle alone - facilitators are there to help

## What Your Facilitator Can Do

### For Clocking Issues
- Reset the scanner connection
- Check your fingerprint enrollment
- Provide backup authentication methods
- Manually record attendance if needed

### For Scanning Issues
- Help with document preparation
- Assist with app settings
- Provide alternative upload methods
- Troubleshoot technical problems

### For System Issues
- Restart equipment
- Check internet connections
- Contact technical support
- Provide manual workarounds

---

## Emergency Procedures

### If Clocking Completely Fails
1. **Tell your facilitator immediately**
2. **Manual attendance** - They can record your attendance by hand
3. **Try again later** - Technical issues are usually temporary
4. **Alternative methods** - Ask about backup authentication

### If Scanning Completely Fails
1. **Keep your documents safe** - Don't lose the originals
2. **Try again later** - Technical issues are usually temporary
3. **Alternative submission** - Ask about other ways to submit documents
4. **Manual process** - Facilitators can help with alternative methods

- **Technical problems happen** - Don't worry, there are always backup solutions
