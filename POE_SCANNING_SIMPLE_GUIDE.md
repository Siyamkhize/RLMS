# POE Document Scanning - Simple Guide

## What Is POE Scanning?

POE stands for "Portfolio of Evidence." This system lets you use your phone's camera to scan paper documents and turn them into digital PDF files that get saved in your learning records.

Think of it like using your phone as a photocopier, but much smarter - it automatically makes the documents look clear and professional.

## How It Currently Works

### The Basic Process
1. **Select your name** from the learner list
2. **Tap "Start Scanning"** to open the camera
3. **Scan each page** by pointing your phone at the document
4. **Review the scanned pages** to make sure they look good
5. **Upload the document** to save it in the system

### What Happens Behind the Scenes
- Your phone's camera takes pictures of each page
- Special software automatically improves the image quality
- All pages get combined into one PDF file
- The PDF gets uploaded to the main server
- Your facilitator and assessors can then view your documents

## Best Practices for Great Scans

### Before You Start
- **Find good lighting** - Near a window or under bright lights
- **Clean your camera lens** - Wipe with a soft cloth
- **Organize your documents** - Put pages in order
- **Find a flat, dark surface** - Table with dark tablecloth works best

### While Scanning
- **Hold phone steady** - Use both hands, brace against something
- **Fill the frame** - Document should take up most of the screen
- **Avoid shadows** - Don't let your phone cast shadows on the document
- **Scan continuously** - Don't take long breaks between pages

### For Best Results
- **Good contrast** - Dark text on light paper works best
- **Flat documents** - Smooth out wrinkles and folds
- **Clean documents** - Remove dirt, eraser marks, coffee stains
- **Proper orientation** - Keep text right-side up

## What Your Facilitator Should Know

### Helping with Scan Quality
- Check lighting conditions in scanning area
- Provide dark surface for document placement
- Help position documents properly
- Review scanned documents before submission

### Troubleshooting Common Issues
- **Blurry scans** → Better lighting, steadier hands, clean lens
- **Dark scans** → Move to brighter area or use phone flashlight
- **Crooked pages** → New system will fix this automatically
- **Missing pages** → System will warn about gaps in page numbers

### When to Call for Help
- App crashes repeatedly during scanning
- Scanned documents consistently poor quality despite good conditions
- Upload failures that persist after multiple attempts
- System showing error messages about file size or format
