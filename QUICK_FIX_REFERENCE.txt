┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE BUG                                                                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  Moderators were seeing ALL learners with POE instead of only learners from
  their allocated classes.

┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE FIX                                                                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  File: get_learners_with_poe_assigned.php
  Line: ~370 (in getAvailableLearnersByStrata function)
  
  Changed:  FROM learnerdetails l
  To:       FROM temp_poe_learners tpl
            INNER JOIN learnerdetails l ON tpl.learnerID = l.LearnerID
