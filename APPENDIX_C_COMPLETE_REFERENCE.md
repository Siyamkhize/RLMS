## Section 1: Trade Overview (9 Categories)

### 1. SAFETY
**Knowledge Points:**
- Standard construction industry safety principles and concepts
- First Aid application and awareness
- Identify different colour markings and symbolic safety signs
- Risk assessment theories and principals and application thereof

---

### 2. HAND, POWER AND WORKSHOP TOOLS
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of Trade related tools and machinery
  - Spanners, Screw drivers, Hammer and chisel
  - Saws and grinders, Trade related Tin-snips (left, right and straight)
  - Drilling machine, Pipe threader (manual and machine)
  - Forming/rolling machine, Guillotine
- Correct use and care thereof

---

### 3. MEASURING EQUIPMENT
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of Trade related measuring equipment
  - measuring tape and steel rule, Spirit level
  - Dumpy Level and Staff, Callipers (inside and outside)
  - Compass and divider, Pressure tester, Chalk line
- Correct use and care thereof

---

### 4. PLANS AND DRAWINGS
**Knowledge Points:**
- Read and interpret Trade related plans and drawings
  - Terms and definitions
  - Signs and symbols
  - Material lists
  - Orientation and levels
  - Existing services

---

### 5. IDENTIFICATION OF PIPE AND FITTINGS
**Knowledge Points:**
- Understand the physical properties and characteristics of various pipe and fittings
  - Copper, uPvc, Galvanised Mild steel, HDPE, Cast Iron, Pex, Plastic
- Correct use and application of the different types thereof
  - When to use screwed, capillary and compression joints

---

### 6. SITE ASSESSMENT
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of how to conduct a site assessment
  - Orientation of site
  - Position of services
  - Position of sanitary ware
  - Position of wastes and drains
  - Liaising with main contractor

---

### 7. RISK ASSESSMENT
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of how to conduct a risk assessment
  - Reduce to writing
  - Use of a checklist
  - Safety file
  - Training
  - Inspections
  - Certifications

---

### 8. SEPTIC TANK
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of the installation and maintenance of a septic tank
  - Excavation, Building / installation of pre-cast tank
  - Invert levels, Access points
  - Backfilling, Compacting
  - Site clearing, Commissioning, Maintenance

---

### 9. SHEET METAL
**Knowledge Points:**
- Knowledge of sheet metal fabrication
  - Different types of joints
  - Templates
  - Developments
  - Cutting and shaping
- Correct use of tools

---

## Section 2: Evaluation Criteria - 5.1 KNOWLEDGE (25 Subsections)

### 5.1.1 Safety
- Knowledge of PPE safety wear
- Precautions to be taken when working in confined areas
- Precautions to be taken when working at heights
- Safe working areas
- Housekeeping
- Use of machines and fitted safety guards

### 5.1.2 Hand, Power and Workshop Tools
- Identify, use and care of the different hand tools
- Identify, use and care of workshop equipment and machines
- Identify, use and care of various hand power tools

### 5.1.3 Measuring Equipment
- Identify, use and care of different measuring devices
- Correct application of different methods of measure

### 5.1.4 Plans and Drawings
- Recall terms and definitions relating to plumbing drawings
- Interpret relevant signs, symbols and abbreviations
- Understand scales and tolerances
- Identify existing services

### 5.1.5 Pipe and Fittings
- Different physical properties of various piping materials and fittings
- Different characteristics of various piping materials and fittings
- Correct use and application of different pipes and fittings
- Understanding the different sizes, grades and class of pipes

### 5.1.6 Transportation, Handling and Storage of Materials
- Correct transportation of materials to site
- Safe handling of materials on site
- Secure storage of materials on site

### 5.1.7 Access Equipment
- Identify, use and care of ladders
- Identify use and care of scaffolding
- Ability to set up and secure access equipment

### 5.1.8 Hot Water Systems
- Explain and allow for expansion in hot water systems
- Understand pressure ratings and limits
- Identify colour codes for pressure ratings
- Identify different types of hot water geysers and boilers including solar
- Identify and correctly position and install the required valves and safety devices

### 5.1.9 Cold Water Systems
- Explain and prevent freezing (contraction) in cold water systems
- Understand pressure ratings and limits
- Identify colour codes for pressure ratings
- Identify and correctly position and install the required valves and safety devices

### 5.1.10 Rain Water Systems
- Ability to install gutters and downpipes as per drawing/instruction
- Ability to install flashing as per drawing/instruction

### 5.1.11 Above Ground Drainage System
- Ability to correctly install waste pipes and drains
- Understanding the gradient (fall) of branches and drain pipes
- Understanding of the functions and installation of branches, vents, traps and inspection eyes

### 5.1.12 Below Ground Drainage Systems
- Ability to correctly install drains and traps
- Understanding gradient (fall) of drain pipes
- Set-out, excavate and bed the drain pipe trench
- Correctly lay and join drain pipes
- Identify acceptable backfill material and methods
- Understand the difference between a manhole and an access chamber

### 5.1.13 SANS Codes and National Building Regulations
- Name the SANS codes relevant to the plumbing trade
- Interpret the relevant SANS codes
- Understanding of the National Building Regulations
- Understanding of the municipal by-laws

### 5.1.14 Sanitary Ware
- Different trade related sanitary ware products
- Water saving devices
- Ability to plan, prepare and install according to instructions
- Colours, textures and finishes

### 5.1.15 Trenching and Backfill
- Understand the relevant regulations
- Be able to set-out and excavate as per the drawing or instruction
- Apply correct shoring methods
- Use the correct backfill medium
- Compact the backfill correctly per requirements

### 5.1.16 Basic Building Works
- Perform basic building works ie. Manhole and inspection chamber
- Identify the different mortar mixes and concrete strength requirements
- Mark and do wall chasings
- Excavations and backfill per regulations

### 5.1.17 Valves and Terminal Fittings
- Identify and name various valves and terminal fittings used in the trade
- Correctly install valves and terminal fittings
- Advise on processes of maintenance of valves and terminal fittings

### 5.1.18 Hydraulic Loading and Air Test
- Correctly calculate hydraulic loads
- Explain flow velocities and the dangers of overloading
- Setting up and use of an air pressure-tester related to drains
- Monitoring and recording of results

### 5.1.19 Fitting and Reading of Water Meters
- Correctly install and maintain water meters
- Explain the different types of water meter
- Understand regulations and requirements regarding water meters

### 5.1.20 Brazing and Soldering
- Explain the function of and describe the different fluxes
- Use a gas flame for soldering and tinning
- Use an oxy-acetylene flame for brazing

### 5.1.21 Jointing and Laying of Piping
- Explain the different characteristics of pipe types and fittings
- When and how to use screwed, capillary and compression jointing methods
- Securing and protection of pipe work from the elements

### 5.1.22 Site Assessment
- Ability to relate physical site with the plan or drawing
- Orientation and position of appliances
- Identifying existing services
- Liaising with the main contractor

### 5.1.23 Risk Assessment
- Report reduced to writing
- Use of a checklist
- Use of a safety file
- Ability to conduct training, inspections and toolbox talks

### 5.1.24 Septic Tank
- Understand and describe the processes involved in the installation of a septic tank from plan to commissioning
- Explain how it works and the importance of invert levels
- Ability to recall the codes of practice related to a septic tank installation

### 5.1.25 Sheet Metal Work
- Use of different jointing methods
- Use of LH, RH, Straight tin-snips for different shapes
- Use of tools and machinery to fabricate gutters, down-pipes and flashing
- Ability to draw a development of a sheet metal pattern to form a 3D object

### 5.2.4 Above Ground Drainage System
- Install a single stack system from a simple drawing

### 5.2.5 Brazing and Soldering and Pipe Jointing
- Make up a series of pipe configurations as per a drawing

### 5.2.6 Below Ground Drainage System and Trenching and Backfill
- Mark out and lay a drainage system as per a drawing

## Section 4: Evaluation Criteria - 5.3 WORKPLACE

**Scope of assessment:**
- Processes and procedures for installation and testing of above ground soil waste and vent systems and sanitary ware appliances
- Processes and procedures for installation and testing of below-ground drainage systems and performing basic building work
- Procedures and processes for installation and maintenance of cold water and hot water systems
- Procedures and processes for installation and maintenance of rain water systems
